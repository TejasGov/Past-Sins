  • 24 secure cells proposed across two floors, plus two glass-door special rooms per floor. Cells are 6.5 × 3.25 m bays; wing support rooms occupy the remaining bank depth.
  • Keep the four-metre fenced perimeter strip clear. Staff stations have views into wing thresholds; patrol routes remain within their assigned wings.
  • Stair connections are planned routes; lift is closed until functional. Every secure room retains an ordinary escape/access route for development testing.
