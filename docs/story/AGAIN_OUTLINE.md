# PAST SINS: "Again"

Story outline v1, 2026-10-16. Direction: the memory-wipe loop, with the Tank drowning as its emotional core.

## Logline

Parth wakes in cell N03 with no memory and forty scratches under his bunk. Every time he's caught, a hidden lab wipes him and the prison stages his first day all over again. The woman doing it is the sister of the boy he let drown twenty-five years ago, and Parth came here on purpose.

## Theme

**Forgetting is not forgiveness.** Parth wants to forget the eighteen seconds in the tank. Meera can't stop reliving them. NIDRA traps both of them: he's stuck forgetting, she's stuck remembering. The game ends when one of them stops running.

## The truth (hidden from the player until late)

- **The tank, 25 years ago.** Parth (11) and Raman Varma (10) are best friends in a small town. Behind the temple there's an old water tank. Raman, a kid obsessed with maths, invents a breath-holding game from the digits of *e*: go under for **2** counts, come up, **7**, up, **1**, up, **8**. "It never ends, Parth. That's why it's the best number." Parth always forgets the order, and Raman always says: *"Again. You always forget the pattern."*
- **The hand.** On the final 8, Raman's foot catches in a submerged irrigation grate. He grabs Parth's hand. Parth is out of air, panics and pulls free. He surfaces alone.
- **The lie.** Parth tells the adults that Raman went into the tank by himself. Nobody questions an eleven-year-old. Raman's family carries it for 25 years, and so does Parth.
- **Meera.** Raman's sister was six. She keeps a cassette Raman recorded for a school contest, reciting *e*; at the end he turns to someone off-tape and says the line. She grows up to be a neuroscientist and leads **NIDRA**, a state-funded trauma-erasure program sold to prisons as "rehabilitation".
- **Why Parth is here.** He was serving a sentence elsewhere for something unrelated and small. He saw a NIDRA volunteer notice, recognised the lead researcher's name, and **volunteered because it was her**. He came to be punished, or to finally say it, or to have it taken away. He doesn't know which, and that's the wound.
- **The loop.** In early cycles he remembered the tank and confessed. In cycle 12, recorded on tape, he begged her to wipe him again: *"I can't carry it. Make me forget."* She did. She kept going, telling herself she'd stop "when you remember why you came." Neither of them has stopped since. He is now on **cycle 41**.
- **Nothing supernatural happens.** The repeated days are staged: the same keys go back on the rack, the same breakfast is served, guards are told to say the same lines. Raman's "ghost" is a hallucination from the induction drug plus the cassette cue.

## How the loop works

**Diegetic rules**
- Getting caught means guards drag Parth to the HQ restricted lift, down to NIDRA B2, into the chair in Interrogation A. The cassette plays (*"Again."*) and the screen fades. He wakes in N03 at 06:00 on "day one".
- Each cycle adds a tally scratch under the bunk. The player starts on cycle 41, so the first sight is 40 scratches.
- Everything Parth recovers, he scratches onto the wall of N03. Bhosle never reports it. **The wall is the save file.**

**What survives a wipe**
- **Kept:** the shard journal (the wall), codes the player learned, the cycle count, and anything stashed in the toilet cistern of N03 (Act II unlock).
- **Lost:** inventory, keys and cards (back on their racks and lockers), position and time.

**The dose.** Each day at breakfast and dinner, the dietary/medical window in Food Services hands Parth a paper cup with a pill.
- Taking it keeps the screen clean and suspicion normal.
- Pocketing it brings withdrawal: the screen-grade shader drifts, your hands shake while aiming or picking locks, and guards notice you look sick (suspicion builds faster). In exchange, memory shards shimmer into view and the deeper Tank shards can be reached.

**Breath.** The pool gets a breath meter of four segments labelled **2 · 7 · 1 · 8**, 18 seconds in total, and Raman's voice counts them. The game's central memory is about holding your breath, so the controls teach that memory physically.

**Rain** keeps its existing role (25% per day) and covers footsteps through the noise system. Act III forces a monsoon night.

## ACT I: "Day One"

**Buildings:** Cellhouse A (all tiers), Food Services, central yard, Maintenance ground and B1 stair lobby.
**Look:** flat grey daylight, humming fluorescent tubes, damp concrete, the institution at its most banal.
**Player goal:** work out where you are. Get out of the cellhouse.

1. **Cold open.** Black screen, the sound of water, a child counting *"two… seven… one… eight…"*, a hand slipping free. Smash cut: Parth gasps awake in **N03, tier 3, north gallery**.
2. **The cell.** Forty scratches under the bunk. **Shard M01** on the bracketed shelf (existing `memory_001`) gives the clue **2718**.
3. **Bhosle**, at the tier-3 **Guard Quarter** with chai: *"Good morning, beta. Sleep well?"* He'll say exactly this every cycle, and players will notice.
4. **Dayroom key rack, ground tier** (existing `cellblock_key`, `service_key`, `medical_key`). In the story these are trusty work keys.
5. **Food Services.** The breakfast line and the **dietary window**, where a gloved hand gives you a pill (**M03**). The first pocket-the-pill choice.
6. **Maintenance, Tool Issue / Parts.** The 2718 keypad locker (existing) holds the **MAINTENANCE ACCESS CARD** and a note in Parth's own handwriting: *"You've been here before. Don't trust the voice. Pool. Hold your breath."* (**M04**). This is the moment the player realises the note is his.
7. **Act break: the first capture.** The **B1 Service Stair** gate is alarmed (**M05** foreshadows it). Guards take him through HQ, down the restricted lift, into Interrogation A. Behind the observation glass is a woman's silhouette. A speaker plays a child: *"Again."* He wakes in N03. Forty-one scratches.

> The first capture is meant to happen. It teaches the loop and makes getting caught part of the story rather than a failure. If a player somehow avoids it, the dose knocks them out at 21:00 that night.

## ACT II: "The Pattern"

**Buildings:** Medical & Wellness (with the pool), Administration & Intake, Cellhouse B. Act I areas stay open.
**Look:** humid evenings, chlorinated cyan pool light, sodium-orange exterior lamps, the first rain.
**Player goal:** who is doing this, and why does it keep coming back to water?

Act II is non-linear, spread across several cycles. Each capture advances it, and the wall in N03 fills up.

- **Pool annex.** The breath meter appears here. Surface dives give **T1 "Summer"**: the tank, the heat, the rules of the game. Reaching the **drain grate in the deep end** needs a full 18 seconds and a skipped dose, and gives **T2 "Deeper"**.
- **Medical, Infirmary (bed 4).** Waking here after a wipe gives **M09**: Meera unmasked and crying over him. She isn't cold. The **Medical Staff / Records** drawer leads to **Jagran**, the anti-induction vial in **Secure Medicine**. Take Jagran before a capture and the next wipe is shallow, which unlocks the cistern stash and the deepest Tank shard.
- **Medical, Showers / Changing.** Running water triggers **M10**: Parth at 11, soaking wet, telling the adults *"He went in by himself."* Raman's mother screaming. **The lie.**
- **Administration, Visitation** (day 3 of a cycle, 10:00). His mother is in the booth. She talks about the village and says the tank was drained this year. She presses a photo to the glass: two boys at the tank (**M06**). On the back, in her handwriting: *Parth & Raman Varma.*
- **Administration, Intake Records.** Parth's file. **Program: NIDRA, voluntary.** His signature, steady. Witness: **Dr. M. Varma** (**M07**). The player connects Varma, Raman and the doorplates in B2.
- **Administration, Warden Office.** A memo: *"S-07 exceeds the projected cycle count. Recommend protocol termination."* In facility slang, a terminated subject takes the **Ghost Exit**: disappeared off the books through the Maintenance tunnels.
- **Cellhouse B, Special Room A.** Kabir behind the glass: *"This is the forty-first time you've asked me that."* He's been Parth's memory all along (**M08**). He tells Parth that Meera is Raman's sister, and that *"everything in this place drains to the old reservoir."* **Special Room B** next door has been prepared. Its plate reads **S-07**.
- **Act break: T3 "The Hand".** After Jagran and a skipped dose, the deep-grate dive plays the full moment: out of air, Parth pulls his hand free. The player surfaces into the pool gasping, with the controller rumbling. Guards are waiting at the deck. For the first time, Meera speaks in her own voice over the speaker: *"You remembered. You always remember the tank. You never remember why you came."*

## ACT III: "Ghost Exit"

**Buildings:** Security HQ (ground and upper), NIDRA B2, Maintenance B1, the drainage culvert, outside the perimeter.
**Look:** a monsoon night. Sheets of rain, searchlights cutting through it, flooded service levels, emergency red in B2.
**Player goal:** termination is tonight. Get out, or choose not to.

Act III is one continuous night. A capture now restarts the night from 19:00 ("you've lived this night before too"), not the whole loop.

1. **Bhosle's warning.** At lock-in he leaves the chai and, under the tumbler, his own access card. *"They told me to bring you down at two. I'll be slow."*
2. **HQ ground, Evidence / Intake.** Parth's property bag holds Raman's school notebook, with *e* written out to fifty digits in a child's hand (**M11**). The pattern continues past 2718: **2818 28**, which is the restricted lift override.
3. **HQ ground, Cell-Key Storage.** The 69-key register. Optional: take Kabir's key.
4. **HQ upper, Surveillance.** A wall of monitors showing forty grainy cycles of Parth failing in forty different places (**M12**). The routes he found in cycles 17 and 33 reveal tonight's patrol gaps.
5. **HQ upper, Control / Radio.** Optional: kill the three searchlight beams.
6. **HQ upper, Police Chief / Command.** Optional: `cell_master_key`, which frees Kabir from Special Room A. That's needed for the Mercy ending.
7. **NIDRA B2.** Take the restricted lift with code 281828, or the Restricted Egress Stair. **Subject Archive:** forty tapes. Cycle 12 is the one where Parth begs to be wiped (**M13**).
8. **Interrogation A / Observation.** Parth can sit in the chair *voluntarily*. That gives **M14 "Why I Came"**: the day he saw her name and signed up. Then the conversation through the glass, the way she has always spoken to him. The player has to choose what to say.
9. **The Ghost Exit.** **Maintenance B1, Gated Landing** (opened by Bhosle's card), then **Drainage / Sump**, then the flooded culvert under the perimeter wall. Air pockets are spaced exactly **2, 7, 1 and 8 seconds of swimming** apart. **Knowing the pattern is how you survive.** On the final 8, in the black water, a small hand reaches for yours (**T4 "Surface"**).

## What this needs from the web port

Most of it fits systems the port already has: `MemoryManager`, `AccessManager`, the environment cycle, the noise system, the pool.

- **Content as data:** shards, notes, dialogue lines and the daily schedule in JSON (the port brief already asks for this).
- **Cycle state** that survives a "wipe": cycle count, wall entries, learned codes, cistern stash. Everything else resets.
- **A scripted capture sequence** (GSAP camera, HQ lift down to B2, chair, fade) instead of the current failure overlay.
- **Breath meter + drowning** in the pool and culvert, with four segments, plus air-pocket triggers.
- **Dose state** driving screen-grade drift, hand sway and a suspicion multiplier.
- **Daily routine** events on the existing 12-minute clock (count, meals, work, yard, lock-in, visitation).
- **Wall-writing decals** in N03 that accumulate with each shard. Tally-scratch decals.
- **A forced monsoon** night for Act III (weather override).
- **Optional, high impact:** record the player's path in each cycle and play the old runs back as translucent "ghosts" during Act III. *Ghost Exit*, literally.

## Open questions

1. **Setting:** keep it an unnamed state, or anchor it to a named region? Language mix in dialogue (English / Hindi / Marathi)?
2. **What was Parth originally imprisoned for?** It's currently "small and unrelated". It could carry more weight, for example a second death he caused through cowardice, so the pattern repeats.
3. **Meera's fate** in the Let Go and Hold On endings. Does she get her own epilogue beat?
4. **Title:** "Blacksite: Ghost Exit" vs. "Past Sins". The outline uses *Ghost Exit* as the Act III name.
